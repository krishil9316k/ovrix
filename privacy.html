Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

This policy explains what Ovrix ("we", "us"), an independent AI studio from India founded by Krishil, collects through this site and Kaapo, and what we do with it.

1. What we collect

  • Your brief. When you use the contact form: your name, email, the service you need, budget, timeline, how you found us and your message.
  • Project files you choose to share with us for a project.
  • Payments. Handled entirely by the payment gateway (such as Razorpay). We never receive your full card or bank details.

This site does not use advertising trackers.

2. How we use it

  • To reply to your brief, prepare a quote and deliver the work.
  • To send invoices and project updates.

Briefs are stored in our database (Supabase). Our AI assistants, powered by Google Gemini, read new briefs to draft a plan for us. A person reviews every plan and every message before anything is sent to you. We never sell your data.

3. Kaapo

Kaapo edits your video inside your own browser; the video file is not uploaded to us. To write captions it sends the audio, and to plan edits it sends your chat messages and small preview frames, to Google Gemini. We do not keep these; Google processes them under its own terms.

4. Who we share it with

Only the services needed to run Ovrix: hosting (Vercel), our database (Supabase), AI processing (Google Gemini), our private team workspace (Discord) and, for payments, the payment gateway. Each handles data under its own data-protection terms.

5. How long we keep it

We keep briefs and project details for as long as needed to do the work and meet accounting and legal requirements, then delete them.

6. Your choices

You can ask for a copy of what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing hello@ovrix.in.

7. Security

Everything is sent over HTTPS. Our database only accepts new briefs from the public; reading them requires our private keys, and only the people working on your project can see them.

8. Changes

We will update the date at the top whenever this policy changes.

9. Contact

Questions about privacy: hello@ovrix.in.
