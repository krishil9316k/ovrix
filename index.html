Process

How a project moves.

Five stages, in the same order, for every service.

  1. Brief

    Tell us the goal and any references, on a call or through the form.

  2. Quote and scope

    A fixed price, a timeline and what's included, before anything is paid.

  3. Production

    We build, with check-ins at each milestone.

  4. Review

    Two rounds of changes are included in every package.

  5. Delivery

    Final files, source and handoff notes. You own all of it.
