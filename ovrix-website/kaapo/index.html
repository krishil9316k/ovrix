How it works

Three steps, about a minute.

Editing runs in your browser, so your video file stays on your device. Only the sound is sent to the AI to write captions.

  1. Drop your clip

    A talking video, a shop walkthrough, or a few product photos.

  2. Say what you want

    "Gujarati captions, cut pauses, add a hook" or tap Full auto edit.

  3. Export and post

    Watch it, tweak anything with a tap, and download the MP4.

FAQ

Good to know.

Which languages work?

Gujarati, Hindi, Hinglish and English for captions. You can also ask Kaapo to rewrite captions from one of these languages into another.

Is my video uploaded anywhere?

No. Cutting, captions on screen and export all happen in your browser. Only the audio goes to the AI to write captions, and the chat text to plan your edit.

What do I need?

Chrome or Edge on a computer or Android phone works best. Export plays your reel once in real time, so a 30-second reel takes about 30 seconds.

How long can a video be?

Reels up to a few minutes work best in the beta.
