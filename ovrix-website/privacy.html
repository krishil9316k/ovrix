Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This is a starter privacy policy for Virtual Creatives Pvt. Ltd. ("Ovrix", "we", "us"). It is a template, not legal advice — have a lawyer review it before you take real client data or payments, especially if you serve clients in the EU (GDPR) or California (CCPA).

1. What we collect

  • Contact details you submit through our brief form: name, email, project description.
  • Project files you share with us for production (video, brand assets, credentials you explicitly provide).
  • Payment information — handled entirely by our payment processors (Stripe, Razorpay). We never receive or store your full card number.
  • Basic usage data (pages visited, browser type) if analytics are enabled on this site.

2. How we use it

  • To respond to your brief and deliver the service you've requested.
  • To process payments and send invoices/receipts.
  • To improve our services and communicate project updates.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Who we share it with

Only the vendors necessary to run the business: payment processors (Stripe and Razorpay), hosting/storage providers, and email delivery tools. Each is bound by their own data-protection terms.

Kaapo. When you use Kaapo, your video is edited inside your own browser and is not uploaded to us. To write captions, Kaapo sends the video's audio, and to plan edits it sends your chat messages and small preview frames, to Google's Gemini AI service. We do not keep these files; Google processes them under its own terms.

4. Data retention

Project files and contact details are kept for as long as needed to deliver the service and meet accounting/legal obligations, then deleted or anonymized.

5. Your rights

You can request a copy of the data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or request full deletion at any time by emailing privacy@signalstudio.co [replace with your real address].

6. Security

Data in transit is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS. Access to project files and client data is restricted to the team members working on your project.

7. Changes to this policy

We'll update the date at the top of this page whenever this policy changes. Continued use of our services after an update means you accept the revised policy.
